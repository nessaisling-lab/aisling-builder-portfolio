How I got here

I learned computers the hacker way. Getting Debian and a Wi-Fi driver running on a PlayStation 3 took months of books, forums and boards. Aircraft-mechanic coursework at Vaughn College came next and taught me methodical troubleshooting and respect for a checklist; a family emergency paused that degree, and the habits stayed.

I ran a short-term-rental business on my own, then did data analysis and lead generation at Architechne. After Per Scholas IT Support, I joined the inaugural cohort of Pursuit's AI-Native Builder program, where I was technical director and repo admin on a client capstone.

Today I build with Claude Code, Gemini and local models like Qwen. They do the hammering; I choose the metal, make the architecture calls, write the specs, and test every piece before it ships.
