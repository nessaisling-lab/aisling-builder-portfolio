Data integration

I connect the systems a team already pays for, so nobody retypes a list again. The proof so far is a nonprofit's gala; the same work fits any small team that runs on Salesforce.

The work

Migrate and clean up

Spreadsheets, old CRMs and duplicate records, moved into Salesforce.

A dry run on a copy first, then the import, with every record traced to its source.

Sync an outside app into Salesforce

Ticketing, donations, events or forms, into Accounts, Opportunities, payments, Contacts and Campaign Members.

A tested sync, plus import files a Salesforce partner can review before anything changes.

Keep it running

Watching syncs after launch, fixing what breaks, and making small changes.

An alert when a job stops, and a written log of every change.

How I work

  • Tested on invented data before anything touches yours.
  • Keys stay on the server, never in a browser or a spreadsheet.
  • No email or record goes out without your approval.
  • Built with Claude Code; no AI inside the app unless you ask for it.

Proof

The gala capstone: a ticketing-platform import that keeps staff corrections, and a Salesforce sync tested against a stand-in org.
