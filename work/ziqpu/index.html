Transcript

This is an astrology decision tool with two agents. One measures, the other interprets, and keeping them apart is what keeps it honest.

There's no account, no email, and no password. You get an anonymous chart-self, kept on this machine. A hosted reader is optional, and I'm skipping it. This birth moment is made up for the video. With no birth time, the app holds back the rising sign and the midheaven instead of guessing them, and it says what that costs. The birthplace resolves offline, down to its time zone.

It gives me an anonymous handle. Then it benchmarks this laptop and recommends the largest local model that will actually fit: a fourteen-billion-parameter model. Nothing downloads until you say so.

Before any reading, I switch the source to the local template, so nothing is written by a hosted model. Then I run the seeded demo: five real companies, each scored against the chart from the day it went public. Every reading carries a badge naming what wrote it.

Backstage is the measurer's work: every aspect between the two charts, with its orb, and the order the tools ran in, which is fixed in code, not left to a model. The interpreter only reads those measures back, and if you ask whether to buy, it won't tell you.

One agent measures, the other interprets, and neither does the other's job. It's for reflection, not advice.

01What it is

It scores your natal chart against the chart of anything with a date of origin (v1: stocks, by IPO). Measurement and meaning are different programs, so a language model cannot touch the arithmetic.

02How it works

The sequence is fixed in code rather than chosen by the model.

  • Hamun-ana computes positions and aspects and returns structured JSON only. It never interprets.
  • Ungasaga reads those measures back in three beats (measured, meaning, reminder) and declines to give advice.
  • A read-only axum sidecar and an MCP server sit beside the Dioxus desktop UI.

03The hard part

An unknown birth time is honest.

  • With no birth hour, the app withholds the Ascendant and Midheaven rather than guessing them.
  • It benchmarks the machine and recommends the largest quantisation that actually fits, and downloads nothing until you say so.

04What it doesn't do

For reflection, not prediction.

  • Not financial, medical, legal or psychological guidance. The interpreter is built to decline advice rather than disclaim it in a footer.
