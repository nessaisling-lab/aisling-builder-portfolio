01What it is

A desktop terminal that pairs conventional market data with a Hellenistic astrology engine. The thesis isn't that astrology predicts markets. It's that strong investors don't dismiss a data source because it's unconventional; they measure it.

02How it works

Conventional plumbing, held to the same bar as the unconventional part.

  • 12 data APIs and 10 native Rust scrapers feeding 46 SQLx migrations.
  • An axum REST sidecar beside an Iced desktop UI with WGSL shaders.
  • An ephemeris engine validated to sub-arcsecond precision against Swiss Ephemeris and JPL data.

03What it doesn't do

Paper trading only.

  • The +37.06% is verified paper-trade ROI, not real money and not a forecast.
  • Pre-revenue. Presented at Pursuit Demo Day on May 6, 2026, to Pursuit staff, fellow Builders and a Fortune 500 industry reviewer.
