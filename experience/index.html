How I work

Measure, don't estimate.

Figures in my repos are measured and say so. Anything derived is labelled derived; anything unverified says unverified.

Verify against production, not the repo.

Most real defects I have found were invisible from inside the editor: a search that answered a Manhattan address with a Brooklyn building, a failed lookup that kept the previous result on screen.

An absent answer is still an answer.

The honest design often has three states, not two: signed, unsigned but intact, tampered. Collapsing the middle one is how software states something reassuring it has not earned.
