# Experience: an-ki

Scope: the Experience page (`/experience/`), rendered by `Experience`, `Ziggurat`, `Tier`, `Shrine`, `Card` and `ToolCard` in `site/src/main.rs`, with the sky drawn by `site/src/sky.rs`. Mode: Read.
Audience: employers, hiring managers, recruiters, Pursuit staff; often on a phone, a minute or two.
Job: see where I've worked first, with its hardest facts; then the stack; then how I work.
Action: open a job's or a tool's card; drag the ziggurat round to read the whole stack.
Proof: JOBS, JOB_FACTS, STACK, TOOLS and RULES in `content.rs`; every "used in" link is held by a test to a phrase on that build's page.
Constraints: PRODUCT.md (truth before polish, flat version first, client never named, no age-revealing dates); the world is DESIGN.md's (desk clay, petrol, matte); every Sumerian or Akkadian word is checked against a source before it ships.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Heaven and earth, an-ki. The experience is plotted on a sprawling sky chart, the stack stands on the horizon as a ziggurat, and how I work is the ground. Refuses the résumé timeline of rows and the card grid.

OWN-WORLD: A night sky (a pale dawn by day) that follows the reading settings; a lamplit meridian; dashed star tracks; the ziggurat in the desk's clay with the kit's three-face light; a thin strip of earth. Archivo display, Atkinson body, Marcellus SC for the meridian and the Sumerian words, Noto Sans Cuneiform for the signs. Matte.

STORY: Four jobs, newest highest, each a track across the sky crossing the meridian at its star, its facts riding along it. The stack is the ziggurat: Rust at the foundation, AI at the summit, every tool a button with its card. The visitor reads the sky, turns the ziggurat, and lands on the three rules.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Tabs. Title and deck top left in the sky. The meridian a third of the way across, the four jobs crossing it, labels alternating sides, facts along their tracks. The ziggurat low on the horizon at the right, rising out of the earth. The earth strip below with How I work.

FORM: The meridian, Aisling's lock from the second hand (Nov 12), then turned over by her: experience in the sky, the stack as the ziggurat, the rules on the earth. seed:c9d6e49cb184d883.
